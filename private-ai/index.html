Your knowledge is valuable. Accessing it should not be difficult.

Business information is spread across policies, contracts, manuals, technical documentation and operational systems.

People spend time searching, comparing information and preparing documents. Public AI services may not fit the organization's data-handling requirements.

ITBASS Private AI brings retrieval and generation together within an agreed deployment boundary.

01 · Capabilities

What can it do?

Ask questions over your documents

Search and ask questions using information indexed from approved document collections. Source references let users inspect the supporting material.

  • What does our procurement policy require for this purchase?
  • Where is the process for onboarding a new supplier?
  • Which internal documents discuss this requirement?

Generate document drafts

Create draft text using selected information from your organization's knowledge base. Review the generated material and its supporting evidence before it is used or published.

Work within projects

Organize sources and user access by project, so teams keep different collections of business information separate.

Extend to specialized workflows

The platform supports an integration and plugin architecture for business-specific applications. Available connections and their security requirements are assessed as part of each deployment.

04 · Deployment and security

An environment under your control.

Customer-controlled deployment

Designed for deployment on customer-controlled servers or suitable private infrastructure. The application, stored information and inference model are configured around the organization's requirements.

No training on your documents

Indexing and retrieving documents are not the same as training a foundation model. ITBASS Private AI does not require your documents to be used for foundation-model training.

Project access and operational controls

Project-level access controls, protected credential handling and auditing of relevant operations.

Security assessed per deployment

Network connectivity, integrations, model hardware and additional hardening depend on the selected configuration. Restricted-network and stronger security requirements are assessed explicitly, not assumed to work with every integration.

06 · The pilot

A pilot starts with a bounded problem, not an open-ended implementation.

  1. Step 1

    Define the use case

    Choose one business domain and a limited set of approved information sources.

  2. Step 2

    Agree on success criteria

    Define the questions or workflows the system must handle, including accuracy, source verification, latency and data handling.

  3. Step 3

    Qualify and deploy

    Select the infrastructure, model and integrations. Establish access controls and test the deployment.

  4. Step 4

    Evaluate together

    Review representative results, limitations, operating requirements and the decision to expand.

07 · Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does our data have to go to a public AI provider?

No. The intended private deployment uses a model running on infrastructure selected for your organization. External integrations and network traffic are evaluated separately.

Does the system train on our company documents?

Document indexing does not retrain the foundation model. The standard private deployment does not require your documents for external model training.

What documents can we use?

The platform supports text-containing PDF and DOCX documents. Additional formats and OCR for scanned documents are assessed separately.

What hardware do we need?

Requirements depend on the selected model, expected workload and deployment architecture. We size the environment during pilot discovery.

Can it run without internet access?

Restricted-network deployments can be evaluated. Full offline operation depends on how models, updates and integrations are supplied, and must be validated for the chosen configuration.

Can it integrate with SAP, GitHub or other business systems?

The platform has an integration architecture and specialized extensions. Specific connector availability, permissions and compatibility are confirmed before a pilot.

Does it always produce correct answers?

No AI system should make that guarantee. Our approach combines source-grounded generation, validation and human review. Pilot success criteria measure how reliably the system handles your actual use cases.

Is it ready for a company-wide deployment?

We start with a bounded pilot and qualify the intended infrastructure, security and workloads before discussing a wider rollout.
